Welcome to Stardew Voice Tracker!

Quick Start:

  1. Select your current season above
  2. Tap the microphone below
  3. Say "Caught [item]" or "What's left?"

Voice Commands:

  • "Caught the Catfish" - Add items
  • "What's left?" - See needed items
  • "What do I need?" - See needed items
  • "Show progress" - View completion
