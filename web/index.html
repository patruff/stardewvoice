🌾 Stardew Voice Tracker

Track your Community Center bundles with voice!

Welcome to Stardew Voice Tracker!

Voice Commands:

  • "Caught [item name]" - Add items to bundles
  • "What do I need for [season]?" - See seasonal items
  • "Show progress" - View bundle completion
  • "Reset" - Clear all progress

Examples:

  • "Caught the Catfish"
  • "What do I need for Spring?"

👇 Tap the microphone to start!
